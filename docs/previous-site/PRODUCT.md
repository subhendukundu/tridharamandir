# Tridhara Milan Mandir Product Context

## Register

Brand surface. This is a public temple, pilgrimage, seva, guest-house, and local heritage website where design is part of trust-building.

## Audience

- Devotees planning darshan, prasad, rituals, festivals, or seva.
- Families and groups comparing Bankura/Bishnupur temple trips from mobile devices.
- Donors and volunteers looking for credible community impact.
- Cultural travellers who need practical directions, stay options, and context before visiting Panchmura.

## Purpose

Help visitors understand why Tridhara Milan Mandir matters, plan a visit with confidence, and take one clear next action: visit, stay, book a ritual, volunteer, donate, or contact the mandir.

## Homepage Rethink

The homepage should behave like a pilgrim planning surface, not a passive brochure. Above the fold, it must answer four questions:

- Is this the mandir I am looking for?
- Can I visit today and what happens when I arrive?
- Should I come for darshan, stay overnight, or join seva?
- What is the next useful action?

The story of the mandir still matters, but it should follow the visitor decision path rather than block it.

## Brand Personality

Devotional, grounded, hospitable, culturally literate.

## Design Principles

- Sacred clarity: devotional tone without making practical information hard to scan.
- Trust before ornament: dates, timings, directions, booking paths, and contact routes must be current and direct.
- Mobile-first pilgrimage planning: most high-intent visitors will compare routes, timings, phone numbers, and stay details on a phone.
- Intent-first routing: day visitors, overnight guests, donors, volunteers, and festival attendees need different next steps.
- Local specificity: Panchmura, Bankura, Bishnupur, terracotta heritage, Naba Brindaban, and integrated worship should feel concrete, not generic.
- Inclusive access: text contrast, touch targets, keyboard navigation, and clear form states are core quality, not polish.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS landing pages.
- Purple-blue AI gradients and glassy card stacks.
- Vague spiritual stock imagery.
- Decorative UI that hides timings, phone numbers, directions, or booking expectations.
- Stale event calendars presented as upcoming.
