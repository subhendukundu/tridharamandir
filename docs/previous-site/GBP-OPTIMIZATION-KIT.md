**Q: What is the distance from Bishnupur to Tridhara Milan Mandir Panchmura?**
A: Tridhara Milan Mandir in Panchmura is approximately 30 km from Bishnupur (about 50 minutes by car). Auto-rickshaws and shared autos are available from Bishnupur railway station to Panchmura.

**Q: Is there a guest house at Tridhara Milan Mandir?**
A: Yes, Tridhara Milan Mandir has a guest house for devotees. Clean rooms are available on a donation basis, and meals including prasad are provided. Visit tridharamandir.com/guest-house for details.

**Q: What are the darshan timings at Tridhara Milan Mandir?**
A: The temple is open daily from 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Morning aarti is at 5:30 AM, afternoon bhog at 12:30 PM, and evening aarti at 6:30 PM. (Please verify exact timings and update.)

**Q: Is free prasad available at Tridhara Milan Mandir?**
A: Yes, free prasad (bhog) is served to all devotees daily at Tridhara Milan Mandir, Naba Brindaban. The temple serves over 2,000 devotees each day.

**Q: How to reach Panchmura Tridhara Milan Mandir from Bankura?**
A: From Bankura town, Tridhara Milan Mandir is about 25 km (45 minutes by car). You can take a bus or auto from Bankura bus stand towards Panchmura. The temple is near Panchmura More.

**Q: Why is Tridhara Milan Mandir called Naba Brindaban?**
A: Devotees lovingly call Tridhara Milan Mandir "Naba Brindaban" (New Vrindavan) because of its dedication to Radha-Krishna worship and its spiritual atmosphere that reminds visitors of Vrindavan. It is Bankura's own mini Vrindavan.

**Q: What festivals are celebrated at Tridhara Milan Mandir?**
