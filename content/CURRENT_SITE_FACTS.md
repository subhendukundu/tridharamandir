## FAQ (their own answers)
- Timings: darshan 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM weekdays, 5:00 AM – 9:30 PM weekends. Arati at 5:00 AM and 6:30 PM (the integrated Tridhara arati in the evening; "evening arati (6:30–8:00 PM)" in another answer — homepage says 18:30).
- Location: Panchmura, Bankura; 30 km from Bishnupur (45 minutes by car); 180 km from Kolkata (4 hours). Local buses, hired cars, shared trekkers from Bishnupur and Bankura town.
- From Bishnupur: shared trekker from Bishnupur bus stand, ₹30–40 per person, every 30 minutes; private taxi ₹600–800; local buses towards Panchmura; the temple is signposted in the village.
- Prasad: completely free, about 2,000 meals a day, served at 12:30 PM after the midday bhog; everyone welcome. "The temple operates on voluntary donations."
- Stay: on-site guest house, 8 suites, 100 m from the courtyard. Email info@tridharamandir.com to book (or call +91 96091 75202). Rooms include anna-daan meals, temple access and optional terracotta workshops. Also guest houses in Bishnupur (30 km) or Bankura town (45 km).
- Architecture: Nagara style, 45-foot shikhara, Radha-Krishna in the main sanctum, terracotta relief panels (Krishna lila, Dasavatara), tulsi courtyard, shrines for Shiva, Kali and Rama–Sita.
- **Photography is allowed in the courtyards; ask before photographing inside the sanctum; no flash, and no interior photography during arati.**
- Naba-Vrindavan is "an informal devotee honorific, not an official title".
- Best time: October to March; monsoon July–August is green (carry rain gear). Major festivals: Janmashtami (August), Rath Yatra (June–July), Navaratri (October). Weekdays are less crowded than weekends.
- Dress: shoulders and knees covered; remove shoes and leather belts before the sanctum; silence during arati.
- Booking: regular darshan is open to all without booking. Groups of 10+, guest-house stays, special pujas and temple weddings: contact in advance (+91 96091 75202, info@tridharamandir.com).
- **Parking: limited street parking near the temple; the temple does not manage parking. The guest house has designated parking.**
- Access: wheelchair access from the eastern gate with volunteer help at peak hours; ramps and railings; email ahead for help during festivals; accessible restrooms and drinking water.
- Facilities: drinking water, shaded seating and restrooms near the anna-daan hall; lockers for small bags beside the eastern entrance; first aid during major festivals.
- Day trip: Bishnupur's Rasmancha, Jor-Bangla and Madan Mohan temples in the morning (2–3 hours), then Panchmura for prasad at 12:30 and the evening arati at 6:30.
- Volunteering: email info@tridharamandir.com with skills and dates; orientation every second Saturday (kitchen seva, scholarship mentoring, health-camp support). CSR and college NSS groups can register for kitchen or distribution shifts at least two weeks ahead. Remote volunteers help with communications, translation and fundraising outreach.
- Arrive 30 minutes before arati to find a seat and leave footwear at the seva desk.
- Guided tours: volunteer interpreters (Bengali or Hindi) with 48 hours' notice.
- Food: all prasad is sattvic and onion-free; note allergies in your booking email.
- Takeaway prasad: packaged prasadam for elders or shut-in devotees, ₹300 packaging contribution.
- 80G: "actively working on obtaining 80G certification"; receipts within 48 hours of contribution confirmation.
- Impact reports: quarterly by email; join the seva newsletter.

## Marriage and rituals (/services/marriage-and-rituals)
- "Sanctify life events with Vedic rites — from Radha-Krishna kalyanam to Bhoomi Pujan, vehicle puja and sacred thread ceremonies in the mandir complex."
- Temple weddings: Radha-Krishna themed weddings with floral mandap, kirtan processions and prasadam catering for up to 400 guests.
- Sanskar ceremonies: priest services for Annaprashan, Namkaran, Upanayan (Poite), Bhoomi Puja and Griha Pravesh with personalised sankalpa.
- Vehicle and business puja: new vehicles, shops or factories, with Navagraha homa and Lakshmi Narayan archana.
- How: share dates, number of people and traditions → the mandir allocates priests, kirtan groups, decoration and prasad menu → for marriages, identity proof, passport photographs and affidavits as per local municipal rules.
- Schedule: wedding mandap 6:00–11:00 AM and 4:00–9:00 PM daily; sanskar slots weekdays 7:00–10:00 AM, weekends 3:00–6:00 PM; priest consultation evenings 7:30–8:30 PM (appointment recommended).
- Offerings: Temple Wedding Package ₹31,001 (mandap decor, priest dakshina, kirtan group, anna-daan for 200 guests); Sanskar Ceremony ₹7,501 (priest, havan samagri, prasadam; Annaprashan, Namkaran or Upanayan); Bhoomi Pujan ₹5,001 (Vastu Shaanti, Navagraha homa, prasadam); Vehicle / Business Puja ₹3,001 (one-hour ritual with Navagraha sankalpa).
- FAQ: preferred caterers and guest-house partners available; marriages need Aadhaar IDs, passport photos and local registrar forms (volunteers help with notarisation); Bhoomi Pujan at the site or at the temple with symbolic earth from your land; ceremonies in Sanskrit with Bengali or Hindi guidance, regional bhajans possible.
