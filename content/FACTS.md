## Mandir
- Names: Tridhara Milan Mandir; "Naba Brindaban Temple"; "Naba-Vrindavan (Second Vrindavan)"; "Panchmura Milan Mandir"; tagline "Panchmura's Second Vrindavan" (nickname "a blessing, not an official title").
- Hero line: "A mandir where three currents of Hindu devotion meet: Mahadev's stillness, Radha-Krishna's bhakti, and Devi's shakti." / "Where Three Streams of Faith Flow Together".
- Deities: Radha-Krishna (main sanctum), Kali, Mahadev (Shiva linga + meditating Shiva statue), Rama–Sita, Hanuman, Chaitanya Mahaprabhu; Jagannath on the altar; Jagannath/Balabhadra/Subhadra for Rath Yatra. SHAKTA = MAA KALI (arati "culminating before Radha-Krishna and Kali"). NOT Durga.
- Arati: trishul (Shaiva), chakra (Vaishnava), shankha (Shakta); Shaiva damru, Vaishnava mridanga, Devi ululation (ulu).
- Architecture: Nagara style, 45-foot shikhara, inspired by Govind Dev Temple, 28 hand-carved relief panels (Dasavatara, Krishna lila), shegun-beamed ceilings, tulsi mancha.
- Kitchen: terracotta handis; sattvic, onion-free food. Site: "Anna-Daan Prasad: a free midday meal shared by visitors, villagers, and devotees"; FAQ "prasad is completely free"; "serves approximately 2,000 meals daily to all visitors"; started with 600 daily plates in 2022; prasadam "prepared with seasonal produce and terracotta handi vessels" (the site does NOT say the handis are made in Panchmura).
- The site links YouTube @tridharamandir but says nothing about live-streaming the arati: any live darshan is a proposal → [CONFIRM].
- Rules: shoulders and knees covered; no leather in the garbhagriha; phones on silent; no flash during arati.

## Story & work
- Founder: Bhajan Dutta, "Founder & Spiritual Guide".
- 2012–16 Community Visioning Circles; 2016–19 trustees acquired the plot; 2020–21 construction (marble murtis, teak doors); 1 July 2022 consecration on Rath Yatra.
- Anna-daan 2,000+ plates daily; 120+ students supported a year (Sanskrit, music, STEM); quarterly health camps, 500+ patients each, across 15 Panchmura villages, volunteer doctors from Kolkata and Durgapur.
